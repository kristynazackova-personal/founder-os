## 1. Confidence

**[changed] 58% for the full Founder OS scope. 68% for V1 alone.**

The full scope raises the ceiling and the TAM but lowers the probability that everything lands, because a solo founder with a day job is now shipping seven surfaces across 24 months. The right read: high confidence in the wedge, medium confidence in the stack, and a clear decision point at month 5.

**Up from v1**
- TAM roughly doubles. V1 only served the $0 cohort. Stage-scaled subscriptions and memberships also serve the 19.8% of Lovable builders already earning, and V3 serves platforms and vertical B2B founders.
- ARPU on earning merchants rises from $39 to $79 to a realistic $90 to $180 with memberships.
- Three data moats instead of one: verified revenue, channel attribution, product feedback. Each compounds the others.
- Platform white-label creates a plausible $10M+ path and an acquisition route.

**Down from v1**
- Scope. Seven surfaces, four regulated or reputation-sensitive lines (ads, outreach, advances, marketplace).
- Membership A overlaps the crowded AI CMO category; Membership B overlaps AI SDR, which is cooling.
- Bans and deliverability: anything that touches Reddit, LinkedIn, X, or email carries platform risk even draft-first.
- Hiring is now required by month 9, not optional.

**What moves it to 75%**: V1 release criteria met by month 5 (5 founders paying through us, 8 of 20 saying "I'd give you a cut"), plus one build platform in an active partnership conversation by month 8.

**What moves it to 35%**: crawl shows <5% real products without checkout, or Lovable ships a native pricing + launch assistant before V2.

## 2. Market

### Demand
- Vibe coding tools market ~$4.7B in 2026, growing ~38%/yr. Lovable ~$500M ARR mid-2026, ~8M users, 60M+ projects, ~1.2M new projects/week. Replit 50M users, targeting $1B ARR.
- Lovable Build Economy survey (n=14,300, May 2026): 19.8% earn directly from their product, 15.3% via client work, 60.5% not yet monetized but intend to. 8 in 10 intend to monetize. Native payments only went live Feb 2026.
- 80% of Lovable builders are non-technical.

### **[changed]** Segments and sizing
| Segment | Who | Triangulated size (Lovable-centric, order of magnitude) | Served by |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1: Pre-revenue builders | Stage 0 to 1 | 500K to 1M engaged, intent to charge | V1 wedge, take-rate |
| S2: Earning indie founders | Stage 2 to 5, Stripe connected | 200K to 400K (19.8% of paying builders) | Subscriptions, Memberships A and C |
| S3: Vertical B2B founders | Healthcare, restaurants, fashion, services | Low tens of thousands, high ARPU | Vertical tier, Membership B |
| S4: Build platforms | Lovable, Bolt, Replit, Base44, Emergent, Hostinger Horizons | 6 to 10 accounts | White-label |
| S5: Stage 4+ needing capital or exit | Subset of S2/S3 | Thousands | Advances, marketplace |

Confidence on sizes: medium-low; survey is builder-level and Lovable-run. The 500-app crawl and a Bolt/Replit equivalent remain the first validation step.

### Timing
Pre-revenue share is at its peak (payments live 7 months). Build platforms are in an acquisition mood and want GMV stories. AI CMOs are pricing at $99 to $249/mo to founders who cannot afford it, leaving the outcome-priced position open.

## 3. Competition

### **[changed]** Full map across the Founder OS scope
| Layer | Players | Their gap | Our angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Build | Lovable, Bolt, Replit, Base44 | Stop at checkout; no pricing, GTM, product ops | Live inside their ecosystem; own the data they don't have |
| Billing / MoR | Dodo, Polar, Creem, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy | Infra only, price war within 1 point, assume you know the price | Wrap, never build; pricing engine on top |
| AI CMO / distribution | Okara ($99 to $249), Runlo ($65 to $99), Vibiz, Jasper, Surfer | Start at the URL; no payment data; credit burnout; subscriptions to $0 founders | Attribution-tied posts, outcome pricing, stage gating |
| Word of mouth / community | ThreadLift (ours), manual Reddit tools, Devi AI | Not tied to conversions | Fold ThreadLift in; measure signups per reply |
| Social scheduling | Buffer, Typefully, Hypefury | Scheduling, not strategy or attribution | Included inside Membership A, not a standalone |
| Product feedback | Canny, Featurebase, Userpilot, Sprig | Built for teams with PMs; $50 to $300/mo | Widget via our snippet; synthesis for non-PMs |
| Release review / security | Reeve, Lovable security scan, Snyk-lite tools | Security only; no pricing or changelog impact | Ship-readiness bundle for non-technical founders |
| PM tooling / training | Productboard, Linear, Reforge, Maven courses | Assume a PM exists | PM-as-a-service for builders; coaching tier |
| All-in-one "founder OS" | HeyBoss ($40M ARR), Hostinger Horizons, Vibiz | SMB/service focus, opaque credits, no software-founder depth | Software founders, outcome pricing, verified data |
| AI SDR / B2B reach | Artisan, 11x, Lindy, Runlo Eva | Crowded, deliverability, churn | Vertical tier only, founder-sent, volume-limited |
| Ads automation | Vibiz, AdCreative.ai, Meta Advantage+ | Hold budget or fee on spend; no payback data | Guardrail model, their account, auto-pause |
| Revenue-based financing | Pipe, Capchase, Clearco, Stripe Capital | Minimum revenue far above our merchants | Underwrite on our own data, partner capital |
| Exit marketplace | Acquire.com, Flippa, MicroAcquire heritage | Self-reported revenue | Verified metrics, auto data room |
| Services | Fiverr (vibe-coder campaign), agencies | Expensive, unrepeatable | Graduation referrals |

### Threat ranking
1. Lovable bundling pricing + launch (existential for V1/V2 if it happens before month 12).
2. HeyBoss moving from SMBs to software founders (direct Founder OS competitor).
3. Okara adding Stripe read and a stage model (copies the diagnosis).
4. Stripe extending Lemon Squeezy or Atlas into guidance.

## 4. Product scope (final)

**V1 (months 0 to 5): Diagnose, price, charge.** Connect and assess (Stripe read, snippet, GA4), diagnosis screen, stage 0 to 3 KPIs, pricing engine, wrapped checkout (Dodo/Polar), basic attribution.

**V2 (months 6 to 12): Launch, grow, memberships.** Stage ladder 0 to 5, stage-scaled subscriptions, launch sequence, weekly playbook, Membership A (social posts, word of mouth, partner reach), Membership C (feedback, release review, coaching), Lovable/Bolt templates and listings, graduation referrals.

**V3 (months 12 to 24): Platform, vertical, capital.** White-label module for build platforms, vertical B2B tier with Membership B, ads with a guardrail, revenue advances via capital partner, exit marketplace, PM automation expansion.

Non-negotiables across all versions: draft-first for anything that posts or sends; never hold ad budget or customer funds; wrap payment and lending infra, never build it; three numbers per stage, one action.

Full detail in the three PRDs.

## 5. Onboarding, stages, attribution
Unchanged from v1 sections 4 to 6: connect and assess, stage-based KPI ladder, attribution yes / ad budgets no. Summary:
- Onboarding is a diagnosis, not a form. Connect Stripe/Paddle/Lemon Squeezy/RevenueCat, snippet or Supabase, GA4, project link; place in stage with a data-confidence flag; first screen is "You're at $1,400 MRR, growing 9%, churn 11%, fix churn first."
- Stage ladder 0 to 5 with show/hide rules and one action each. Peer bands only at n ≥ 50 per cell.
- Snippet events: source, signup, activation, checkout view, purchase, 30-day return.

## 6. Monetization

### Principle
Charge when they get paid. Take-rate is the acquisition funnel for new builders; subscriptions and memberships are the revenue; V3 lines are the ceiling.

### **[changed]** Revenue lines and target mix at month 24
| Line | Mechanics | Mix |
|---|---|---|
| A. Take-rate | 6% + 50¢ on wrapped checkout vs ~4% + 40¢ MoR cost; ~2-point spread | 20% |
| B. Stage subscriptions | $39 auto-unlock (wrapped) or $29 / $79 / $149 by stage (Stripe-connected) | 30% |
| C. Memberships | A Distribution $49 to $79; C Product ops $39 to $99, coaching $199; B B2B reach $149 to $249 (vertical only) | 25% |
| D. V3 lines | Platform white-label (0.5% GMV or per-merchant), ads guardrail ($99 or 5% of spend), advances (6 to 10% origination), marketplace (5 to 10% success fee) | 20% |
| E. Referrals | Graduation partners, 10 to 20% first-year | 5% |

### Pricing page (V2)
| | Free | Earning | Growing | Scaling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis, stage KPIs | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Pricing engine | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Checkout 6% + 50¢ | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Attribution | Basic | Full | Full | Full |
| Launch sequence | 1 channel | All | All | All |
| Weekly playbook | No | Yes | Yes | Yes + advisor |
| Monthly fee | $0 | $29 to $39 | $79 | $149 |
| Memberships A / C | Add-on | Add-on | Add-on | Included C |

### **[changed]** Projections (base case, full scope)
Assumptions: average merchant $600 MRR GMV; 40% of earning merchants on a subscription; 35% of subscribers take one membership at $60 avg; V3 lines from month 15.

| Month | Active merchants | Take-rate margin | Subscriptions | Memberships | V3 lines | Total MRR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | 100 | $0.8K | $0.6K | $0 | $0 | $1.4K |
| 12 | 800 | $9.6K | $9K | $5K | $0 | $24K |
| 18 | 2,000 | $28K | $24K | $14K | $10K | $76K |
| 24 | 3,500 | $56K | $42K | $25K | $30K | $153K (~$1.8M ARR) |

Sensitivity: the model is most sensitive to average merchant GMV (±50% moves month-24 MRR by roughly ±$40K) and to membership attach (each 10 points is ±$7K). V3 lines are the least certain and are excluded from kill criteria.

### What not to do
No subscription before first dollar; no credits; no holding ad budgets or customer funds; no custom billing or lending infra; no fully automated posting.

## 7. Go-to-market
- Months 1 to 2: 20 founder interviews (r/lovable, Lovable Discord, MentorCruise), 500-app crawl.
- Months 2 to 3: pricing engine free; "I priced 50 vibe-coded apps" content; Product Hunt.
- Months 3 to 5: wrapped checkout private beta, 30 founders.
- Month 6: Lovable and Bolt templates, Connectors listing; launch sequence.
- Months 6 to 12: memberships A and C; partner network seeded from early merchants; first platform conversations.
- Months 12 to 24: platform pilot, vertical tier via founder case studies (ShiftNex-style), capital partner, marketplace.

Acquisition channel priority: build-platform ecosystems, community, content from our own data. Paid only for the vertical tier.

## 10. Risks (full scope)
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Lovable bundles pricing + launch | Be in their ecosystem early; own attribution, partner network, benchmarks; sign a non-Lovable platform first if needed |
| Scope dilution | Strict V1 → V2 → V3 gates; A and C only before B; nothing in V3 before a platform conversation |
| Platform bans / deliverability | Draft-first everywhere; no posting scopes; volume limits on outreach |
| Regulatory (advances, marketplace) | Licensed partners execute; legal review before pilots |
| Ads reputation | Their account, hard caps, auto-pause, stage 4+ only |
| Cohort smaller than modeled | Crawl first; pricing engine works for any indie founder |
| Founder bandwidth | Month-4 decision; hires by month 9 |
| MoR partner risk | Provider abstraction; Dodo and Polar interchangeable |
